How to use it?

The lessons follow NandGame's levels in order. The solutions are hidden in closed boxes: try it yourself first, then look if you get stuck.

  • 01 Open the level on nandgame.com
  • 02 Try it on your own first
  • 03 If stuck, look at the lesson's hints
  • 04 Open the solution only at the very end
